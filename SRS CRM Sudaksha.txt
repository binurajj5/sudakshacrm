Software Requirements Specification (SRS) for Sudaksha Integrated CRM and Operations Management System (S-CRM/OMS)Section 1: Introduction and Contextual Overview1.1 Purpose and Scope of the S-CRM/OMSThe Sudaksha Integrated CRM and Operations Management System (S-CRM/OMS) is specified as a mission-critical platform designed to streamline Sudaksha Education (P) Ltd.'s end-to-end business operations. The primary purpose of this integrated system is to consolidate traditionally disparate functions—marketing, B2B and B2C sales, financial documentation, detailed course management, and logistical coordination—into a single, unified enterprise solution.Sudaksha operates as an online platform offering multi-category courses 1, with a core business focus on higher education, technical, and vocational training.2 The organization adheres to a dual business model, serving B2B institutional clients to "Empower your workforce" and managing B2C direct consumer enrollments.3 The scope of the S-CRM/OMS is defined to manage the entire customer and resource lifecycle, from the initial lead capture, through complex sales solutioning, generation of legal and financial documentation (NDAs, PIs, POs), to the final enrollment and integration with the existing Learning Management System (LMS).The current annual revenue, recorded at ₹3.15Cr for the financial year ending March 31, 2024 2, indicates that Sudaksha is operating beyond the nascent startup phase. This level of operation necessitates robust, enterprise-grade architecture capable of managing complex, long-cycle B2B contracts concurrently with high-volume, quick-turnaround B2C lead pipelines. A generic Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system, such as a basic free tier like HubSpot 4, would inherently lack the necessary deep integration capabilities required for customized financial reconciliation, detailed course versioning, and specialized training management. Therefore, the architectural design outlined in this SRS focuses on building a proprietary system that incorporates elements typically found in Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and Training Management System (TMS) software, tailored specifically to close the operational loop between sales and service delivery.1.2 Business Objectives and Critical Success Factors (CSFs)The success of the S-CRM/OMS will be measured against specific, quantifiable business objectives designed to enhance efficiency, conversion, and operational control.Objective A (B2B Efficiency): To reduce the average sales cycle time for institutional clients. This will be achieved by automating the complex processes of solutioning, drafting financial documentation (Proforma Invoices and Purchase Orders), generating non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) for trainers, and handling bulk enrollment.Objective B (B2C Conversion): To increase lead conversion rates for high-volume B2C leads. This objective relies on implementing automated, compliant multi-channel outreach campaigns and improving the visibility and tracking tools available to sales counselors.5Objective C (Operational Control): To achieve 100% accurate financial tracking and reconciliation. This critical factor requires seamless integration of retail student fee payments and institutional B2B invoice tracking with the existing accounting and financial platform.6Section 2: Analysis of Best-in-Class CRM Features and Benchmarking2.1 Competitive Landscape and Core Educational Feature SetsSpecialized CRMs developed for educational institutions extend far beyond standard sales pipeline management. They are designed to manage the entire student lifecycle, including enquiry, application management, academic tracking, retention, and alumni engagement.5Key features identified across leading education CRMs include: unified student and applicant profiles to eliminate data silos; automated admissions workflows; multi-channel communication capabilities (supporting Email, SMS, and WhatsApp); predictive lead scoring to prioritize high-quality prospects; robust integration with critical systems like Student Information Systems (SIS), Learning Management Systems (LMS), and ERPs; and specialized dashboards for counselors and teachers.5Benchmarking against industry leaders reveals diverse strengths: Salesforce Education Cloud is recognized for its suitability for enterprise-scale institutions; Slate by Technolutions excels at comprehensive admissions management; and Element451 is noted for AI-driven engagement. HubSpot, while strong for marketing-focused teams, is often cited as a free option and may lack the necessary depth for complex operational requirements.4 The S-CRM/OMS must incorporate the marketing and lead nurturing capabilities seen in HubSpot while achieving the deep lifecycle management and integration rigor of more specialized systems like Slate or those built on Salesforce Education Cloud.2.2 Feature Benchmarking and S-CRM/OMS Gap AnalysisA comparison with commercial CRM offerings highlights crucial functional gaps that the Sudaksha system must fill to meet its operational demands, particularly concerning its hybrid B2B/B2C model.The S-CRM/OMS requires significantly higher capability in complex B2B account tracking and institutional sales pipeline management than typically seen in admissions-focused systems like Slate. Furthermore, the requirement for integrated financial documentation (PIs, POs) and the specialized task of cloning customized course solutions demands functions that are generally not native to off-the-shelf CRMs, necessitating extensive customization or dedicated modules.The most prominent gap identified is the need for integrated Training Management System (TMS) functionality. Standard CRMs, even those specialized for education, typically do not natively manage the detailed logistics of sourcing, contracting, and scheduling instructors.7 Sudaksha’s explicit requirement to source trainers, assign them to courses, and generate both NDAs and Purchase Orders (POs) requires the integration of a custom Training Management (FR 4.0) module directly into the CRM architecture. Profile (linking sales, financial, and academic history) and the Course Solution Registry (linking sold curriculum versions to trainer assignments and financial liabilities).Conclusions and RecommendationsThe development of the S-CRM/OMS is a complex undertaking that requires integrating three distinct software domains: a traditional CRM, a robust Content Configuration Manager (CCM/Product Module), and an operational Training Management System (TMS).The analysis strongly supports the decision to build a custom solution, as no single existing education CRM fully integrates the necessary financial documentation capabilities (PI/PO generation) with the logistical requirements of trainer sourcing and assignment, a necessity imposed by Sudaksha’s hybrid B2B/B2C model.The critical path for implementation must focus on the following technical mandates:Identity Management Standard: Prioritize the implementation of SCIM (System for Cross-domain Identity Management) for LMS integration (FR 5.1.2). This is non-negotiable for achieving efficiency and data integrity when handling bulk enrollment required by B2B institutional contracts.Product-Finance Linkage: The architecture must enforce a direct data dependency between the Product and Financial Modules (FR 3.2.2 and FR 4.3.2). All financial documents related to trainers (POs) or clients (PIs/Invoices) must automatically reference the specific, version-controlled Course Solution being delivered, establishing a crucial audit trail for operational accountability.Messaging Compliance: The Digital Outreach Suite must be built entirely around the WhatsApp Business API, with explicit mechanisms for consent tracking and pre-approved template management (FR 1.2.6). Failure to adhere to these protocols risks suspension of bulk communication functionality.Before development commences, the Product Owner should prioritize providing the requested clarification points regarding the specific existing LMS platform and financial system (ERP/Accounting software). These details are fundamental for defining the precise API endpoints and data mapping required for the mission-critical integration workflows.
